# VYSRO Logo Asset

`vysro-logo.webp` is a proportionally resized, lossless web derivative of the locked master logo shown in the approved VYSRO Master Brand System Board.

Controls:

- Do not redraw or regenerate the logo with AI.
- Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, add effects or remove the descriptor.
- Preserve aspect ratio and adequate clear space.
- On dark or complex backgrounds, use a clean white protection plate.
