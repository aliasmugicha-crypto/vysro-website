# VYSRO Website MVP v0.1 — Design Specification

**Status:** Draft for final CEO review  
**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Repository:** `aliasmugicha-crypto/vysro-website`  
**Deployment target:** Netlify  
**Primary device:** Mobile/Android first, responsive desktop second

## 1. Purpose

Build the first deployable public VYSRO website as a serious business front door for technical organisations, especially small and medium enterprises (SMEs) in manufacturing, production, construction, engineering and related quality/compliance environments.

The website must show that VYSRO helps technical organisations improve **operational efficiency, quality, compliance, traceability, evidence and management visibility** by converting real operational knowledge into structured systems.

The MVP must help a first-time visitor understand:

- who VYSRO is;
- what operating problems VYSRO helps solve;
- how VYSRO supports technical SMEs and growing businesses;
- what systems and technical solutions VYSRO can design;
- how standards, engineering, research and evidence strengthen those systems;
- how a technical expert can join the VYSRO Expert Network;
- how an organisation can contact VYSRO for consulting, systems, research or partnership work.

The website must separate VYSRO's stable institutional identity from specific products, systems and offers currently being developed.

## 2. Governing identity

### Official descriptor

**Operational • Intelligence • Systems**

### Primary tagline

**Turning Operational Knowledge into Intelligent Systems.**

### Governing philosophy

**The operation comes before the software.**

VYSRO starts with how work is actually performed: requirements, people, processes, standards, evidence, controls, risks and management decisions. Digital tools are introduced only where they improve control, traceability, visibility or decision quality.

The website should communicate the following operating logic:

**Operational event → structured evidence → applicable control or escalation → management decision → assigned action → verified result → feedback and continual improvement.**

A shorter visual version may be used throughout the site:

**Process → Evidence → Control → Intelligence → Value**

Supporting message:

**Better Evidence. Better Control. Better Decisions.**

## 3. Core market positioning

VYSRO should be presented as an **operational-intelligence and technical-systems consultancy for technical organisations**, with particular relevance to SMEs that cannot justify expensive enterprise software or large consulting programmes.

Priority environments include:

- manufacturing;
- production;
- construction;
- concrete and materials businesses;
- engineering services;
- quality and compliance functions;
- field operations;
- standards-driven technical organisations.

The central commercial problem is not “lack of software.”

It is that many technical businesses operate with fragmented spreadsheets, paper records, WhatsApp communication, disconnected forms, weak visibility, inconsistent quality controls and limited management intelligence.

VYSRO helps organise those operations into practical systems using fit-for-purpose tools, standards, technical controls, evidence and management dashboards.

The website must therefore avoid positioning VYSRO as a software vendor selling expensive proprietary platforms.

## 4. Design benchmark philosophy

The visual and information architecture should combine the strongest qualities observed in three approved benchmark directions:

- **Genesis Analytics:** clear institutional identity, Africa-rooted positioning and disciplined presentation of multidisciplinary work.
- **iTad:** evidence-led storytelling, measurable outcomes and a strong relationship between evidence and decision-making.
- **CSIR South Africa:** technical depth across engineering, research, systems and applied innovation.

VYSRO must not imitate or reproduce proprietary layouts or copy. These references are strategic benchmarks only.

## 6. Primary audiences

The homepage and navigation should serve five primary visitor groups:

1. **Technical SMEs and growing businesses** seeking better control, efficiency and visibility without expensive enterprise systems.
2. **Manufacturers and production businesses** seeking production, QA/QC, traceability and management systems.
3. **Construction and engineering organisations** seeking technical assurance, project controls, standards and operational systems.
4. **Institutions and research/standards organisations** seeking evidence, technical research, data systems and standards support.
5. **Experts and specialist consultants** seeking opportunities to join the VYSRO Expert Network.

## 8. Homepage structure

The homepage should tell a coherent business story in this order.

### 8.1 Hero / institutional identity

The first screen should make clear what VYSRO does.

Recommended content direction:

**VYSRO**  
**Operational • Intelligence • Systems**

**Turning Operational Knowledge into Intelligent Systems.**

Supporting message:

VYSRO helps technical organisations — especially SMEs in manufacturing, production and construction — improve operational efficiency, quality, compliance, traceability and management visibility through practical, evidence-driven systems.

Primary CTA: **Improve Your Operations**  
Secondary CTA: **Join Our Expert Pool**

Use the real approved VYSRO logo exactly as supplied. Do not redesign or substitute it.

### 8.2 The problem we solve

A short section should describe the practical reality of many technical SMEs:

- fragmented spreadsheets;
- paper-based controls;
- disconnected forms;
- weak production visibility;
- inconsistent quality records;
- delayed management information;
- poor traceability;
- standards that exist on paper but are not embedded in daily work.

The message should not criticise SMEs. It should show that VYSRO converts existing operational knowledge into structured, usable systems.

### 8.3 How VYSRO works

Use a simple visual operating sequence:

**Process → Evidence → Control → Intelligence → Value**

Supporting principles:

- processes before software;
- evidence by design;
- controls embedded in work;
- management information generated from operations;
- practical technology matched to business capacity;
- continual improvement.

Use only claims that can be evidenced. Initial candidate metrics:

- **1,122** registrations in the SizAfrica pilot;
- **9** pilot districts;
- **215+** standards supported/developed/reviewed through relevant professional experience — wording must distinguish institutional experience from individual/team experience;
- no extrapolated “countries impacted,” CO₂ reduction, economic value or market-impact numbers until separately evidenced.

### 8.5 Industries / operating environments

Show where VYSRO's methodology applies:

- manufacturing;
- production;
- construction;
- concrete and materials;
- engineering;
- standards and quality;
- field operations and MEL.

Message:

**Different technical environments. One accountable operating philosophy.**

### 8.6 Featured systems

- Precast Production System;

### 8.9 Insights / publications

## 11. Expert Network page

This is a primary business funnel.

The page should answer:

- Why join VYSRO's expert network?
- Who should register?
- What kinds of assignments may experts be matched to?
- How does matching work?
- What information is collected and why?
- How is personal information handled?

Initial expert intake fields:

- full name;
- email;
- phone / country;
- professional title;
- discipline / expertise categories;
- years of experience;
- countries/regions of experience;
- sector experience;
- languages;
- LinkedIn / professional profile;
- availability;
- CV upload;
- consent to retain/process information for opportunity matching.

CVs and personal records must **never be stored in the public GitHub repository**.

For v0.1, expert intake may use Netlify Forms with a CV file-upload field, subject to verification of current upload/security limits during implementation. Submissions must remain outside the public repository and notifications must be configured to a designated VYSRO inbox. The public form must include explicit consent/privacy wording before submission.

## 12. Privacy, confidentiality and claims governance

- Do not publicly name operational clients or companies unless the user explicitly approves that disclosure.
- Do not use private client logos, screenshots, operational data or project documents.
- Public named case studies must have a defensible public basis and approved attribution.
- Every quantitative claim must be traceable to evidence before launch.
- Distinguish VYSRO institutional achievements from founder/team prior professional experience.
- Clearly label synthetic dashboard data as demonstration data.
- Do not present future KOSH/VERA/AI functions as completed capabilities.
- Personal expert information must be collected outside the public source-code repository and handled under an explicit consent/privacy model.

## 13. Visual direction

- preserve the actual VYSRO logo and brand assets exactly;
- mobile-first Android experience;
- clean, technically credible, African/international consulting aesthetic;
- strong typography and white space;
- number-led evidence sections;
- dashboards, maps, diagrams, charts and technical interfaces as core visual assets;
- selective real project/research photography where available and authorised;
- AI-generated contextual imagery may be used only where appropriate and must not imply a fabricated real project, client or field event;
- avoid dependence on autoplay video or large media assets;
- fast loading on mobile connections.

The website should look like a serious technical operating partner, not a generic software startup.

## 15. Technology baseline

For MVP v0.1:

- static HTML5;
- CSS3;
- minimal vanilla JavaScript;
- no frontend framework unless a concrete requirement emerges;
- Netlify deployment from GitHub;
- Netlify-compatible expert intake;
- no production KOSH/VERA backend in this release;
- no authentication/account system in this release;
- no confidential database in GitHub.

This keeps the first release fast, understandable, inexpensive and maintainable while leaving room for future application modules.

## 16. Definition of MVP success

A first-time visitor on an Android phone should be able to:

1. understand within the first screen that VYSRO helps technical organisations improve operations through practical intelligent systems;
2. recognise manufacturing, production and construction SMEs as a core target market;
3. understand VYSRO's philosophy: operations before software, evidence by design and better management decisions;
4. see credible quantitative and project evidence without inflated claims;
5. understand how standards, engineering, research and systems support operational improvement;
6. view concrete demonstrations of VYSRO systems without client confidentiality breaches;
7. register / join the expert pool through a clear route;
8. contact VYSRO for consulting, systems, research or partnership work;
9. navigate and read the site comfortably on a mobile connection;
10. distinguish existing work from capabilities under development.

## 17. Explicitly out of scope for v0.1

- production KOSH/VERA implementation;
- vector database / RAG / LLM application;
- client portals;
- authenticated expert accounts;
- automated opportunity matching;
- tender/bid automation;
- payment systems;
- full Engineering Office application;
- private operational dashboards;
- autonomous decision systems.

These may become later modules once the public business front door and expert-intake funnel are operating.

## 18. Governing homepage impression

The website should leave a first-time visitor with this understanding:

> **VYSRO helps technical organisations turn the way they already work into better-controlled, evidence-driven operating systems — improving efficiency, quality, compliance, traceability and management decisions without requiring expensive enterprise technology.**

Standards, engineering, research and digital tools strengthen that mission. They are not disconnected service lines.
