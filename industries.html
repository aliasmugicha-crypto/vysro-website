SME principle

Operational maturity can grow in stages.

A small technical business does not need to imitate a large enterprise on day one. It needs a controlled information architecture that can scale as the operation grows.

Stage 1 — Make work visible

Map process, responsibility, evidence and daily performance.

Stage 2 — Embed controls

Define thresholds, approvals, escalation and corrective action.

Stage 3 — Build management intelligence

Turn controlled evidence into trends, decisions and improvement priorities.

Stage 4 — Integrate and automate

Connect the proven operating model to ERP, applications or automation where justified.
