Privacy

How VYSRO handles expert-profile information in this MVP.

This notice explains the purpose and boundaries of information submitted through the public Expert Network form and related website enquiries.

Why we collect expert information

VYSRO collects expert-profile information for expert-profile administration and potential opportunity matching. Registration helps us understand professional disciplines, experience, geography, sectors, languages and availability.

What we collect

The MVP form may collect your name, contact details, country, professional title, discipline, expertise, years of experience, regions and sectors of experience, languages, LinkedIn or professional profile, availability and consent.

CV handling

CV file upload is not enabled in the ordinary public form. Where a CV is needed, VYSRO will request it through an approved secure channel. Expert personal information and CVs are not published through GitHub.

What registration means

Submitting a profile expresses interest in the network. It does not guarantee an assignment, contract, tender role or payment. VYSRO may contact a registered expert where their profile appears relevant to a potential assignment and will apply additional verification and consent controls where required.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell expert information. Information is used for legitimate VYSRO expert-network administration and potential assignment matching, subject to the consent and controls applicable to the relevant opportunity.

Retention and deletion

VYSRO is developing a formal expert-data retention schedule. This MVP does not invent a fixed retention period. Retention, review and deletion controls will be documented and applied as the Expert Network operating system matures.

Data or privacy requests

For access, correction, withdrawal or other data or privacy requests, use the VYSRO contact page and identify the request as privacy-related.
