Privacy Notice

Expert Network & website form data.

This MVP privacy notice explains how VYSRO intends to use information submitted through its public expert and business forms.

Why we collect information

Expert information is collected for expert-profile administration, capability classification, communication and potential opportunity matching. Business-enquiry information is collected so VYSRO can understand and respond to the enquiry.

What we may collect

Depending on the form, this can include identity and contact information, professional title, disciplines, expertise, work regions, sectors, languages, availability and professional-profile links.

CVs and uploaded files

CV upload is not enabled through the ordinary public form in this MVP. CVs and other personal files will be requested only through an approved secure channel. Personal information is not published through GitHub.

Opportunity matching

Information may be used to identify experts whose stated capabilities appear relevant to a potential assignment. Registration does not guarantee an assignment, inclusion in a bid, employment or contracting.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell expert information. Where an opportunity may require sharing profile information with another party, VYSRO should contact the expert and apply the relevant consent and submission controls before use.

Retention

VYSRO has not published a fixed retention period in this MVP. A formal retention schedule will be adopted as the expert registry matures. Data should not be retained longer than necessary for the stated purpose.

Data or privacy requests

For data or privacy requests, use the VYSRO Contact page. A dedicated privacy contact route may be added in a later release.
