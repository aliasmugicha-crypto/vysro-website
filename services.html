Services

Technical operations translated into controlled, decision-ready systems.

VYSRO works at the intersection of standards, engineering, quality, operations, evidence and digital workflow design. The objective is not software for its own sake; it is better operational visibility, stronger control and better decisions.

01

Operational Intelligence Systems

Systems that convert operational events into structured evidence, metrics and management intelligence.

  • Operational process and event mapping
  • KPI and threshold definition
  • Exception, escalation and decision logic
  • Daily, weekly and monthly management routines
02

Standards-Embedded Workflow Design

Translation of standards, specifications, contracts and QMS requirements into process steps, validation rules, approvals and records.

  • Clause-to-process mapping
  • Control-point design
  • Roles, approvals and evidence requirements
  • Traceability and audit logic
03

Quality, Inspection & Evidence Systems

Digital and procedural controls for inspection, non-conformity, corrective action, approvals, traceability and audit readiness.

  • Inspection and test plans
  • NCR/CAPA workflows
  • Evidence registers and approvals
  • Corrective-action verification
04

Production & Operational Control

Visibility across production, maintenance, downtime, capacity, resources, quality, waste, cost and dispatch.

  • Production planning and actuals
  • Downtime and maintenance evidence
  • Quality and yield variation
  • Dispatch and resource control
05

Technical Reporting & Decision Support

Decision-ready reports, dashboards, registers, controlled workbooks and management-review information.

  • Executive and operational dashboards
  • Controlled performance registers
  • Management-review packs
  • Decision and action tracking
06

Systems Architecture & Implementation Support

Process maps, functional requirements, user roles, data structures, acceptance logic and developer handoff for ERP and operational applications.

  • Functional requirements
  • Data and role architecture
  • Prototype and workflow validation
  • Acceptance and implementation controls
