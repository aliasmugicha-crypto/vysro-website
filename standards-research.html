Flagship evidence case

SizAfrica anthropometric evidence

Problem

Sizing and product standards require representative anthropometric evidence. Imported assumptions can fail to reflect the population a standard is intended to serve.

Intervention

A structured anthropometric evidence programme combining field methodology, digital data capture and standards-development support.

Method

Defined methodology, field controls, digital collection, validation, analysis and traceable evidence suitable for technical review.

Scale

1,122 pilot registrations9 pilot districts

Figures are attributed specifically to the pilot in accordance with the public claims register.

Broader lesson

Locally generated evidence can strengthen standards by connecting technical requirements to the people, products and environments they are intended to serve.
